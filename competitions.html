03 / COMPETITION RULES

KNOW THE
RULES.

The following are basic competition guidelines. Participants should follow any additional rules provided by the organisers before the event.

01

REGISTRATION

Competitors must complete the application form before the registration closing date.

02

ARRIVAL

Competitors should arrive early for registration, preparation and stage call times.

03

PERFORMANCE

Performers must remain within their allocated performance time and follow stage instructions.

04

MUSIC

Competitors are responsible for submitting the correct music in the required format.

05

COSTUMES

Costumes should be appropriate for the performance category and suitable for movement.

06

SPORTSMANSHIP

All competitors must show respect towards judges, organisers, performers and spectators.

07

SAFETY

Performers must ensure that their routines and movements are safe for themselves and others.

08

JUDGING

Judges may consider technique, timing, musicality, presentation, creativity and overall performance.
