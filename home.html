About Legends Are Made

Legends Are Made Dance is a dance and entertainment competition that provides performers with an opportunity to showcase their talents. The organisation hosts competitions covering different performance categories, including Latin and Ballroom dancing, Freestyle, Singing/Vocals and Performance Cheer, as well as other performance categories. Its target audience includes dancers, singers, performers, dance studios, teachers, judges, parents, families and spectators.

Mission

To provide performers with opportunities to showcase their talent and passion on stage.

Vision

To create an exciting and inclusive platform that celebrates performers and the performing arts community.
