01 / THIS IS LEGENDS ARE MADE

MORE THAN A
COMPETITION.

Legends Are Made Dance Series is a platform created to give dancers, singers and performers the opportunity to showcase their talent.

The competition brings together performers, dance studios, teachers, families and spectators in an exciting environment where passion, creativity and performance are celebrated.

Whether you are stepping onto the stage for the first time or you have years of experience, Legends Are Made gives performers a place to express themselves and grow.

OUR MISSION

CREATE OPPORTUNITIES. CELEBRATE TALENT.

To provide performers with opportunities to showcase their talent, creativity and passion on stage.

OUR VISION

BUILD A STAGE FOR EVERYONE.

To create an exciting and inclusive platform that celebrates performers and the performing arts community.
