ABOUT LEGENDS ARE MADE

Legends Are Made Dance Series is a dance and entertainment competition that provides performers with an opportunity to showcase their talents.

The organisation hosts competitions covering different performance categories, including Latin and Ballroom dancing, Freestyle, Singing/Vocals and Performance Cheer.

The competition brings together dancers, singers, performers, dance studios, teachers, judges, parents, families and spectators.

MISSION

To provide performers with opportunities to showcase their talent and passion on stage.

VISION

To create an exciting and inclusive platform that celebrates performers and the performing arts community.

COMPETITION CATEGORIES

Latin

Latin dance performances.

Ballroom

Traditional and modern ballroom performances.

Freestyle

Creative freestyle performances.

Singing / Vocals

Vocal and singing performances.

Performing Cheer

Cheer, movement and performance routines.
